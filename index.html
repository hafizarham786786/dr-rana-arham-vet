Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our free 24/7 online emergency care, video consults, and pet shop delivery.

Is the online consultation really 100% free of cost? +
Yes. Dr. Rana Arham Ali provides 100% free telemedicine consultations and emergency guidance as part of an ethical animal welfare commitment to companion animal parents worldwide.
How quickly will Dr. Arham respond to late-night emergencies? +
Emergency inquiries marked with "🚨 Late-Night Emergency" receive top priority response on WhatsApp. If Dr. Arham is active, you will receive an immediate response or video call link within minutes.
Can I share blood tests, X-rays, or photos of injuries? +
Absolutely. Once you connect via WhatsApp, you can directly send ultrasound images, radiographs, CBC/biochemistry reports, or videos of your pet's mobility for review.
How does ordering and delivery from the Pet Shop work? +
Browse items in our Pet Shop, add them to your cart, and click "Order via WhatsApp". Your complete order is sent directly with your address. We dispatch orders across Islamabad, Rawalpindi, and nationwide with Cash on Delivery (COD) or bank transfer.
Can online consultations replace in-person veterinary surgery? +
Telemedicine is ideal for urgent guidance, first-aid, post-operative care, and nutritional adjustments. However, if your pet requires physical palpation, emergency anesthesia, or surgical intervention, Dr. Arham will guide you on the safest way to transport your pet to a local clinic.
